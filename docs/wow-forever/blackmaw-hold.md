# Blackmaw Hold

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Checked 31 October 2026 · Announced five-player dungeon · Official level band: 55–60.** Preview information is based on announcements, demo reports and client records.

## Location and access

Azshara. A furbolg city with groves and mountain tunnels affected by corruption. [Official overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever).

Warcraft Tavern reports the tunnel mouth at Azshara 38.7, 33.4, with the portal deep inside at Winterspring 66.6, 84.4. Treat these as preliminary approach coordinates. The site explicitly says the interior has not been mapped. [Dungeon research source](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/blackmaw-hold/).

## Bosses and drops

Varrox Bleakhoof is tied to the dungeon’s Legacy completion criterion. Daybreak also identifies Crimson Wrest: client text says it forces players toward Varrox and prevents action. This is a datamined ability clue, not a tested strategy or complete encounter script. Other mechanics, the complete roster and boss drops are unverified.

Use the reported Azshara tunnel approach for orientation, then verify current portal access. No verified dungeon layout image exists in the reviewed sources; a world-zone map does not show the interior.
