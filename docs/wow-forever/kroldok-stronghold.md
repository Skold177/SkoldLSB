# Krol’dok Stronghold

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Checked 31 October 2026 · Announced five-player dungeon · Official level band: 40–45.** Preview information is based on announcements, demo reports and client records.

## Location and access

Riverglades. An ogre fortress containing camps, workshops and inner corridors. [Official overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever).

Warcraft Tavern reports a cave west of the main fortress gates at Riverglades 37.5, 37.5, and an uphill route through buildings toward a wooden bridge. These are preliminary access/layout reports, not a verified launch walkthrough. The official level range is 40–45, correcting older 40–55 listings. [Dungeon research source](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/kroldok-stronghold/).
