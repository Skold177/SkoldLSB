# Stratholme Service Gate

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Research snapshot: 31 October 2026.** Tactics and annotated layouts use Classic references. Forever loot reports and Classic drop tables are listed separately.

**Level:** Client level 55; Classic planning 58–60. **Location:** Eastern Plaguelands. **Group:** 5. Client level is a listing value, not a promise that a group at that level can finish the dungeon. [Forever listing](https://playfulls.com/loot-tables/dungeons/stratholme).

## Before entering

Classic reference: Key to the City from Magistrate Barthilas opens the service entrance. Assign fear/possession handling and disease removal. The Baron rescue timer is a separate dungeon-set objective; agree on it before crossing the gate. Deathcharger's Reins is a Classic reference drop; Forever availability/rate is unconfirmed. [Classic preparation reference](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/stratholme-undead-dungeon-strategy-wow-classic).

## Layout and route

![Stratholme Service Gate: annotated Classic reference layout](maps/stratholme-service-gate-reference.jpg)

Annotated Classic reference layout; Forever details may differ. [Full-size map](https://eintr.net/WoW/images/Stratholme-walkthrough-map-1024x781.jpg) / [Map source](https://eintr.net/WoW/World-of-Warcraft-Classic-Dungeon-Walkthrough.html).

Enter the service gate on the eastern approach. Defeat Anastari, Nerub'enkan and Maleki and clear acolytes inside all three ziggurats. Kill Barthilas if encountered. In Slaughter Square, clear every abomination to trigger Ramstein, survive the follow-up waves and Black Guard, then enter Rivendare's chamber. [Classic route reference](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/stratholme-undead-dungeon-strategy-wow-classic).
