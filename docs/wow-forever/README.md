# World of Warcraft: Forever dungeon guides

Research snapshot: **31 October 2026**. This collection covers all nine announced new dungeons and 26 returning Classic dungeon or wing entries. Scarlet Monastery, Dire Maul, Blackrock Spire and Stratholme are split into separate guides where routes and encounters differ.

Each guide includes access and preparation, boss tactics, linked item records and layout references wherever supporting evidence is available. Thirty guides include embedded layouts (31 images, including both Dalaran floors). The five announced previews identify missing boss-drop and interior-map evidence explicitly.

## Reading the evidence

- **Beta guide:** documented Forever beta encounters and reported drops. Details can change before launch.
- **Announced preview:** announcements, demo reports or client records; a complete encounter guide, boss-specific loot and interior maps remain unverified.
- **Returning guide:** Forever community reports supplemented by labeled Classic references. Classic loot, optional encounters and routes are not confirmation of their Forever behavior.
- **Levels:** the new-dungeon bands follow the official overview. Returning entries distinguish planning ranges, client listing levels and reported entry minima where those differ.
- **Loot:** item links lead to the relevant database record. Reported drops do not establish a drop rate. Vendor items, chest rewards and quest items are identified separately where the source allows; missing records do not establish that a boss has no loot.

The guides synthesize linked sources; they were not verified through an in-game run or packet captures. The research does not cover raids. Map credits and original image URLs are preserved beside each layout and in the [map source register](maps/README.md).

## Source approach

The [official dungeon overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever) establishes the new-dungeon roster and level bands. Encounter and access details use cited guides from Icy Veins, Method, Warcraft Tavern and community research. Playfulls provides community loot reports and client-derived listings; ClassicDB and Wowhead Classic support the explicitly marked Classic reference tables. Citations appear next to the claims and tables they support so later beta changes can be reconciled locally.

Maps are existing attributed layouts, including fan recreations for the new dungeons and Classic references for returning dungeons. Boss pins can be incomplete or approximate. Consult each map's source and the guide's route notes together.
