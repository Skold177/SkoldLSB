# The Drowned City

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Checked 31 October 2026 · Announced five-player dungeon · Official level band: 35–40.** Preview information is based on announcements, demo reports and client records.

## Location and access

Offshore west of Grom’gol, Stranglethorn Vale. An emerged troll city with naga and trolls; the approach begins underwater. [Official overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever).

Warcraft Tavern reports the entrance near 21.4, 27.9, beyond the Vile Reef at Sunken Hollow. Its BlizzCon-demo account describes a cavern ascent into a central pool area with side sections. Treat this as preview evidence, not a verified launch route. [Dungeon research source](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/the-drowned-city/).

## Bosses and drops

Warcraft Tavern reports four names from the BlizzCon demo: Zul’Alai, Var’Taka, Zin’aka and Captain Dreadrise. Daybreak separately ties Min’loth the Serpent to the dungeon’s Legacy completion criterion. This is a partial roster with mixed demo/datamined evidence, not an established encounter order. Individual mechanics and boss-to-item drops remain unverified.
