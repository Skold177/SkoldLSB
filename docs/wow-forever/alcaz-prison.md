[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Checked 31 October 2026 · Announced five-player dungeon · Official level band: 48–53.** Preview information is based on announcements, demo reports and client records.

## Location and access

Alcaz Island, Dustwallow Marsh. A prison occupied by Defias and naga. [Official overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever).

The island location is official. The wiki documents a five-player instance record and a Blazeroar kill statistic in the beta client, while carefully separating that evidence from playable access. [Dungeon research source](https://wowfwiki.com/wiki/Alcaz_Prison).
