# Shaper’s Terrace

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

**Checked 31 October 2026 · Announced five-player dungeon · Official level band: 58–60.** Preview information is based on announcements, demo reports and client records.

## Location and access

Northern hills of Un’Goro Crater. A titan facility overlooking Un’Goro. [Official overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever).

Warcraft Tavern reports an entrance above Marshal’s Refuge at Un’Goro 48.1, 6.1. Its preliminary layout description includes an open area, tar pit, central Pillar of Assimilation and eastern crystal tunnel. Blizzard confirms that Un’Goro power crystals can be used at consoles. [Dungeon research source](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shapers-terrace/).

Bring Un’Goro power crystals as Blizzard advises. Use the reported hillside entrance only as a preview reference. Crystal quantities, console order and a verified interior layout image remain unknown.
