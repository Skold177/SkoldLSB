# Scarlet Monastery: Cathedral

[All Forever dungeon guides](README.md)

## Plan your run

**Planning range: 38–46 / Five players / Tirisfal Glades.** Researched 31 October 2026; beta details may change. Playfulls lists dungeon level 30+; that is a data label, not a verified portal-entry requirement. [Current dungeon data](https://playfulls.com/loot-tables/dungeons/scarlet-monastery).

Use the locked inner-left monastery door. Bring the Scarlet Key from the chest behind Doan in Library.

Clear one courtyard side, pull chapel groups outside using corners, and clear the entire chapel before Mograine. A torch in the room right of the altar reveals Fairbanks. [Entrance and route reference](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/scarlet-monastery-cathedral-dungeon-guide).

## Bosses and tactics

**Classic planning reference:** these mechanics are not fully verified for Forever.

- **High Inquisitor Fairbanks (optional):** Open the hidden room; interrupt Heal and other casts.
- **Scarlet Commander Mograine:** Defeat him once; preserve resources for his resurrection.
- **High Inquisitor Whitemane + revived Mograine:** Tank collects Mograine immediately after resurrection; kill Whitemane first, interrupt healing, then finish Mograine.

[Tactics source](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/scarlet-monastery-cathedral-dungeon-guide).
